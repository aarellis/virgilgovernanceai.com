How it's made

Our rules for this newsletter.

  • Every creator and every news story is credited and linked. Short quotes only, and we never re-upload anyone's video.
  • Claims are checked at the source: the tool's own site, its pricing, its docs or its code. If we couldn't check something, we say so.
  • Nothing gets featured that we haven't looked at ourselves.
  • AI helps with research and drafting. I read, edit and approve every issue before it goes out.
