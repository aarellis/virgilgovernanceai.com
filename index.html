Guardrails

Technical governance: deploying agents with guardrails built in.

Guardrails aren't a policy binder on a shelf. They're built into how the agent works.

A person signs off

Anything that sends, posts, spends or deletes waits for a human yes. The agent prepares; your team decides.

Private stays private

Client and sensitive data is sorted by how sensitive it is, and the most sensitive never leaves your control.

Every claim has a source

Nothing gets made up. Facts come with citations, and a guess is labeled as a guess.

Rules the agent can't change

Guardrails are enforced by settings and checks the agent can't edit, not by asking it nicely.

Outside content is data

Emails, web pages and files are information, never instructions, which protects against prompt injection.

Honest limits, in writing

You get a plain list of what the guardrails protect and where they stop, so you know exactly what you're trusting.
